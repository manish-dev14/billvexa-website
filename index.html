Frequently Asked Questions

What is BillVexa?expand_more
BillVexa is a cloud-based billing, POS, and inventory management platform built for Indian retailers, wholesalers, and small-to-medium businesses — covering GST invoicing, stock control, purchases, and reporting in one place.
Who is it for?expand_more
Kirana stores, supermarkets, pharmacies, electronics shops, garment stores, wholesalers, distributors, restaurants, and pretty much any Indian business that bills customers and tracks stock.
When will it launch?expand_more
We're actively in development. Join the waitlist below and you'll be the first to know when beta access opens.
Will there be mobile apps?expand_more
Yes. Native Android and iOS apps are on the roadmap, right after public release, so you can bill and check stock from anywhere.
Does it support GST?expand_more
Fully. CGST, SGST, and IGST are calculated automatically, with HSN-based rates and filing-ready GST reports built in from day one.
Can I migrate from Tally?expand_more
Migration tools for moving your product catalog, customers, and opening stock from Tally and similar software are planned as part of onboarding.
Does it work offline?expand_more
BillVexa is cloud-first. Limited offline billing with automatic sync once you're back online is on our roadmap for a future update.
