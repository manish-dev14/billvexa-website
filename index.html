Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy a new computer?expand_more
No. BillVexa runs in the browser, on whatever computer, laptop or mobile you already have. If you want to use a barcode scanner, a USB or Bluetooth one will work.
Will I get reports for GST returns?expand_more
Yes. The GSTR-1 and HSN summary can be downloaded as JSON and Excel and sent straight to your CA.
Is there a limit on products or bills?expand_more
No. Products, inventory and bills are all unlimited.
How long is my data kept?expand_more
Your data is kept safe for 3 years.
Can I manage staff salaries too?expand_more
Yes. Salary, advances, payments and the monthly payroll are built in, with reports.
Can my staff make bills?expand_more
Yes. Give each person a role — owner, manager, cashier or accountant — with its own permissions and a PIN login.
What happens after the free trial?expand_more
If you like it, you continue on the Growth plan. We don't take card details for the trial, so nothing is charged automatically.
Is internet required?expand_more
Yes. BillVexa runs in the browser, so you need an internet connection to use it.
